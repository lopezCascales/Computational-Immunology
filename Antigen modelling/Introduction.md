
Antigen and antibody-antigen complex modeling uses computational tools to predict 3D structures and binding interactions from sequence data

### Key Approaches and Tools

  #### AI Folding and Structure Generation: 
  Tools like ImmuneBuilder generate antibody structures rapidly from sequences, while AlphaFold and AlphaFold-Multimer predict protein-protein interactions and complex structures, achieving near-native success rates that improve with increased sampling.
  
  #### Integrative Molecular Docking: 
  Software like HADDOCK3 performs information-driven docking using known epitope or paratope restraints to model antibody-antigen and nanobody-antigen complexes.
  
  #### Composite and Refinement Pipelines: 
  Traditional and hybrid methods combine separate structure prediction with rigid-body or local refinement tools like ClusPro, RosettaAntibody, and SnugDock.
